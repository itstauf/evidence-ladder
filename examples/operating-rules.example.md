### [CUSTOMER]

#### Buying a better tool never fixes a team that keeps failing the same way | tier: `auto-apply`
**Rule:** When a team has abandoned two tools at the same step, redesign that step before
proposing a third tool. Do not sell a tool into the same failure.
**Context cue:** Operations teams of 20 to 150 people with repeated abandonment at one step.
Not when the earlier tools failed for unrelated reasons.
**Sources:** 3 distinct. Earned in: `learnings.md`
**Last changed:** 2026-08-21 (promoted)

#### A named internal owner by week two predicts renewal | tier: `auto-apply`
**Rule:** Ask for a named internal owner before week two. Flag the account as at risk if
none exists.
**Context cue:** Operations teams of 20 to 150 people buying a first internal tool.
**Sources:** 3 distinct accounts. Earned in: `substrates/customer-profile.md`
**Last changed:** 2026-08-02 (promoted)

#### The buyer who says "we tried a tool already" is the best fit | tier: `suggest` (under review)
**Rule:** Ask early which tool they tried and where it broke. Treat a fully abandoned prior
tool as a positive qualification signal.
**Context cue:** Service businesses with front-desk or dispatch teams; prior tool fully abandoned.
**Sources:** 2 distinct accounts. Earned in: `substrates/customer-profile.md`
**Last changed:** 2026-09-06 (promoted)

### [SALES]

#### "Who will own this after you leave?" is the real price objection | tier: `auto-apply`
**Rule:** When a buyer says it costs too much, answer ownership after handover first. Do not
open with a discount.
**Context cue:** Buyers with no prior internal tool and no in-house developer.
**Sources:** 3 distinct deals. Earned in: `substrates/sales-objections.md`
**Last changed:** 2026-07-19 (promoted)

#### A live demo beats a written proposal for operations buyers | tier: `suggest` (under review)

**Context cue:** Only where a single operations lead can say yes. Not for boards or committees.
**Sources:** 3 supporting, 1 contradicting, distinct deals. Earned in: `substrates/sales-objections.md`
